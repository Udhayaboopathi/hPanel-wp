Restoring replaces this site's current files and database with the backup's contents. Before anything is overwritten, RK Host automatically creates a fresh safety backup of the site as it is right now — if that safety backup fails, the restore does not start. The restore itself runs as a tracked deployment job, and its recorded checksum is verified before extraction.
